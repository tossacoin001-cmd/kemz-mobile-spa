Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Where do you offer your services?

We’re based in Oniru, near Lekki Phase 1, and come to homes, hotels and event venues across Lagos. Send your location when booking and we’ll confirm availability.

Are you really available 24/7?

Yes. Early mornings, late nights and weekends are all bookable. For the best chance at your preferred time, book a few hours ahead.

What do I need to prepare?

Just a quiet space with room for your therapist to work. Let us know about any allergies, injuries or skin sensitivities in the booking notes.

Can you come to my hotel?

Absolutely. Many of our clients book us during business trips and stays. Just include the hotel name and your room details.

How much does it cost?

Single treatments start from the prices shown on each treatment, and packages are priced per person. Prices cover Lekki, Victoria Island and Ikoyi; a small transport fee may apply elsewhere in Lagos. We confirm the full price on WhatsApp before your appointment.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes. Special discounts are available for multiple treatments and recurring appointments. Ask us when you book.

Can I book for a group or special occasion?

Yes. Tell us how many people and the occasion (birthday, bridal shower, girls’ day in) in your booking notes and we’ll plan it with you.
